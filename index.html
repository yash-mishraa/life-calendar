    Tap the downloaded APK and select Install. (If prompted, allow "Install unknown apps" in Settings).

    Tap "Apply Wallpaper"

    Tap the black Apply Wallpaper button. The app measures your phone screen and sets both Lock and Home screen wallpapers with automatic local midnight updates.

  1. 1

    Option A: Native Lock Screen & Home Screen Widget

    Open the included ios-app in Xcode or install the companion widget. It refreshes at 12:00 AM local time automatically using Apple's WidgetKit.

  2. 2

    Option B: 1-Minute Apple Shortcuts Automation

    On iOS, third-party apps cannot change system wallpapers silently in the background without Apple Shortcuts automation:
    • Open native Shortcuts on iPhone → tap Automation.
    • Tap + → Time of Day → set to 12:00 AM Daily.
    • Select Run Immediately (disable "Notify When Run").
    • Add action: Set Wallpaper with your downloaded Life Calendar photo.

  3. 3

    Done

    Every night at 12:00 AM sharp, your iPhone quietly steps to the new day without touching a button.
